The three Kay-Twelve shot archetypes

Each archetype maps to a named set of Nano Banana defaults. When Claude Code receives a brief, it identifies the archetype and applies these settings automatically.

The Space

Wide establishing shot. Shows the full learning environment — architecture, furniture layout, natural light. Students may be present but are secondary to the space itself. Used for: project reveals, website hero, LinkedIn articles, newsletter headers.

Camera defaults
camera_model: "Canon EOS R5"
lens: "24mm"
aperture: "f/5.6"
iso: "800"
framing: "wide_shot"
angle: "eye_level"
focus_point: "whole_scene"
lighting.type: "natural_sunlight"
film_stock: "Kodak Portra 400"
Composition rules
  • Shoot from corner or doorway — show sightlines
  • No clutter in foreground
  • Center-weighted — subject centered for multi-ratio crop
  • Natural light source visible or implied

The Moment

Close action shot. One student engaged with the space — reading, writing, thinking, interacting with a piece of furniture in a creative or unexpected way. Authentic, not posed. Used for: Instagram primary feed, storytelling posts, blog in-article images.

Camera defaults
camera_model: "Nikon Z 8"
lens: "85mm"
aperture: "f/1.4"
iso: "400"
framing: "medium_shot"
angle: "eye_level"
focus_point: "face"
lighting.type: "natural_sunlight"
film_stock: "Kodak Portra 400"
Composition rules
  • Subject centered, generous head room
  • Background softly blurred (bokeh) — space visible but not distracting
  • Expression: engaged, natural — never posed/smiling at camera
  • At least one piece of furniture/environment visible

The Collaboration

Group shot showing students working together — discussion, problem-solving, hands-on activity. Captures energy and purpose. Space is equally important as students. Used for: LinkedIn feed, Facebook, newsletter, blog headers.

Camera defaults
camera_model: "Nikon Z 8"
lens: "50mm"
aperture: "f/2.8"
iso: "400"
framing: "full_body"
angle: "eye_level"
focus_point: "whole_scene"
lighting.type: "natural_sunlight"
film_stock: "Kodak Portra 400"
Composition rules
  • 2–4 students max — avoid crowd feel
  • Students facing each other, not camera
  • Activity visible — something in their hands or on the surface
  • Space tells the story equally — furniture, materials, layout visible
Universal negative prompts (all archetypes)
