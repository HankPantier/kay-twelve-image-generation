The Pattern — Two Distinct Shot Types
Environmental / Wide Shots

Lens range: 17–35mm

Aperture: f/4–f/8 (everything sharp)

ISO: Higher (800–3200) — ambient light

No flash — preserves natural lighting mood

Used for: establishing shots, showing full space, LinkedIn/website hero images

Action / Student Shots

Lens range: 50–85mm

Aperture: f/1.2–f/2.8 (subject isolation)

ISO: Low (200–400) — clean, sharp

No flash — natural, authentic feel

Used for: Instagram close-ups, storytelling moments, newsletter headers
